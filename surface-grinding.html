Why Homeowners and Businesses Choose Polyurea Floor Coatings

Concrete is one of the most durable building materials available — but it also has one major weakness. It’s porous.

Without protection, concrete absorbs water, oil, grease, chemicals, road salt, dirt, and moisture. Over time, these contaminants stain the surface and contribute to deterioration.

A professionally installed coating seals the concrete and creates a protective layer that’s attractive, durable, and easy to maintain.

Our multi-layer coating system is designed to withstand everyday wear while providing a clean, finished appearance for years.

Our Premium Floor Coating System

Every ProCoat floor is installed using proven preparation methods and premium materials.

Step 1

Professional Concrete Surface Preparation

The quality of a floor coating depends on how well the concrete is prepared.

We mechanically diamond grind the surface using professional equipment to remove contaminants, coatings, weak concrete, and surface imperfections.

This process opens the concrete pores, creating the ideal profile for maximum adhesion. Unlike simple acid washing, mechanical grinding provides a much more consistent surface for the coating system.

Proper preparation is one of the biggest differences between an average floor and one built to last.

Step 2

Polyurea Base Coat

Once the concrete has been properly prepared, we apply our industrial-grade polyurea base coat. Because polyurea remains more flexible than many traditional coatings, it performs well under changing temperatures and heavy use.

Polyurea is known for its:

  • Excellent adhesion to prepared concrete
  • High flexibility
  • Impact resistance
  • Abrasion resistance
  • Fast cure time
  • Long-term durability

Step 3

Full Broadcast Decorative Flake

While the base coat is still active, decorative vinyl flakes are broadcast across the entire surface. This creates a textured finish that offers several benefits:

  • Attractive appearance
  • Added slip resistance
  • Hides minor imperfections
  • Creates visual depth
  • Available in numerous color blends

Whether you prefer a modern gray blend, a warm tan, a bold black-and-white look, or a custom color combination, we have options to match nearly any home or commercial space.

Step 4

Polyaspartic Clear Coat

The final layer is a premium polyaspartic clear coat. This protective finish seals the decorative flakes while providing excellent durability and UV stability.

Benefits include:

  • Protection against sunlight
  • Chemical resistance
  • Excellent abrasion resistance
  • Easy cleaning
  • Gloss or satin finish options
  • Long-lasting beauty

The result is a seamless, professional floor that’s designed to perform in demanding environments.

Why Surface Preparation Matters

Many coating failures begin long before the coating is applied. Poor preparation often leads to peeling, delamination, bubbling, and premature wear.

At ProCoat, we believe preparation is the foundation of every successful installation. That’s why we use professional diamond grinding equipment — not shortcuts — to properly prepare every floor before coating begins.

The extra time invested in preparation pays dividends in long-term performance.
